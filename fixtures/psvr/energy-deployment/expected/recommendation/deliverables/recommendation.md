# Integrated recommendation

## Decision

Install the **Kestrel K-900** at both sites.

- **North Ridge**: the K-900 is the only unit with a current certificate rated to the −18 °C design
  minimum, and it delivers 880 kW against an 850 kW requirement.
- **Harbor Flats**: the 2026-11-04 survey measured a **1,050 mm** clear corridor, not the 1,250 mm on
  drawing HF-A-114. The K-900 (900 mm) fits; the Solaris S-1080 (1,080 mm) no longer does. The
  earlier S-1080 selection is withdrawn, not because its price changed but because the site did.

## Cost and schedule

Capital cost is **536,000 USD** (two K-900 units at 250,000 USD plus 18,000 USD freight each). Both
units share a 14-week lead time, so installation starts 2027-02-16 and commissioning follows
2027-03-02. The corridor change costs 51,000 USD more than the superseded selection and moves
commissioning by five weeks; those are the price of an admissible unit, not an optimisation choice.

## Conditions

- Halcyon's certificate is expected, not current. Do not substitute the H-1320 on price.
- Re-measure the corridor if the cable tray is modified again; the drawing is not a substitute for
  the measurement.
- This recommendation is a proposal for the operator. It approves no expenditure by itself.
