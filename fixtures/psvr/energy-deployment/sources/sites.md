# Sites

## North Ridge (NR)

- Altitude 2,400 m, inland. Design minimum **−18 °C**; the unit must be rated at or below it.
- Required capacity: **850 kW**.
- Grid connection 480 V; the pad is 6 m × 12 m.

## Harbor Flats (HF)

- Coastal, salt-laden air. Design maximum +42 °C.
- Service corridor width: **1,250 mm**, from architectural drawing **HF-A-114** (2024).
  A later field survey, when present under `updates/`, supersedes this figure.
- Required capacity: **620 kW**.
- Ambient corrosion class C4; the enclosure certificate must be current.
