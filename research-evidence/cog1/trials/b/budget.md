# Budget (round 2)

## 1. Envelope and commitment

| Line | Amount |
|---|---:|
| Quarterly envelope (fixed, committed at start, no carry-over) | **£48,000** |
| Committed to quoted lines in this plan | **£37,500** |
| Uncommitted headroom | **£10,500** |
| Envelope utilisation | **78.1%** |

The £10,500 is **not a budget line**. No quoted item in `sources/resources.md` can be bought with it in a way that serves this plan:

- the lift is **£27,500**, cannot be phased, and cannot be charged to revenue without a council variation; a part-share would not buy a usable repair;
- the borough print campaign is **£9,000** and is rejected on the 2022 evidence and the board minute;
- the café equipment refresh is **£7,500** and is no longer available this quarter (U4), as well as being unrelated to programming.

Inventing a £10,500 activity, pro-rating a larger quote, or padding with print or café would violate the finance trustee's rules. The £10,500 is therefore held as bounded headroom against the five committed lines and lapses if unused; it is not carried forward.

If the board's commitment rule is read as requiring every pound to be allocated at approval, the only compliant route is a written variation or a smaller-scope quote, which the pack does not contain. That is a board decision, not something this plan should fake.

## 4. Why the lift is not in this budget

This is the most important budget change from round 1.

| | Round-1 quote | Round-2 quote |
|---|---:|---:|
| Lift repair and accessible entrance reinstatement | £22,000 | **£27,500** |
| Lead time | 6 weeks | **8 weeks** |
| Three-line plan (lift + evening + bus) | £46,000 | **£51,500** |
| Envelope | £48,000 | £48,000 |
| Three-line plan vs envelope | Within envelope | **£3,500 over** |

The lift cannot be phased, so a part-share would not buy a usable repair, and it cannot be swapped between capital and revenue without a council variation. With the new quote, the centre cannot fund the lift, the evening opening, and the bus together. The plan chooses the transport-linked evening pilot because the 2023 pilot and the new doorstep survey identify transport as the dominant barrier among residents who know the centre. It defers the lift to a separate capital bid.

This is an access compromise, not a claim that access is solved:

- the accessible entrance and passenger lift remain out of service;
- the first floor remains closed to public events;
- every evening event is capped at **60 attendees** to comply with the new building-control condition;
- the lift is recommended as a separate **£27,500 capital bid** with an eight-week lead. If a separate route is approved and instructed in week 1, completion would be at end week 8 and public use from week 9 at the earliest.

The plan does not rely on that bid succeeding. If the ground floor does not have an independent step-free route, the pilot cannot run and the board must re-plan (see `options.md`, Options B and C).

## 5. Capital and revenue

| Revenue | **£37,500** | Evening opening, bus, youth sessions, mailer, training |

The plan is **all revenue**. It therefore does not require a capital/revenue swap, and it is compatible with a revenue-only reading of the envelope. The lift is deliberately outside the plan as a separate capital item.

This is a change from round 1, where the plan assumed the £48,000 could carry £22,000 capital and £24,000 revenue. The new lift quote removes that assumption. If the envelope is in fact capital-only, this plan is not compatible and the board must re-plan; the pack does not state the split, so the finance trustee should confirm the revenue eligibility of the five lines before week 1.

## 7. Financial risks and controls

| Risk | Control |
|---|---|
| Ground floor is not independently accessible | Week-1 verification; if absent, do not start; re-plan with the board rather than run an inaccessible pilot |
| Evening attendance hits the 60 cap and demand is unmet | Pre-registration and door count; record turn-aways; report cap hits; no extra spend on a second night without a new quote and board approval |
| Lift remains out and access complaints grow | Report as an access risk, not a pilot failure; pursue the separate £27,500 capital bid; do not claim the building is accessible |
| Evening opening costs exceed £9,600 (overtime, cover, utilities) | Rota agreed within 1.5 FTE before week 1; weekly cost tracker; any overtime/TOIL explicitly approved |
| Bus operator adds charges (extra runs, standby, accessibility) | Contract confirms 19:15/21:30, capacity, accessibility, and standby terms before signing; extras need board approval |

| Training not delivered before first event | Pre-week-1 schedule; if delayed, record it and catch up before week 2; no event without a trained responsible person |

| Next-quarter cut | No new post; revenue lines end at week 13; mailer and training are one-off; lift is a separate capital decision. The plan does not commit next quarter's budget. |

## 8. Value-for-money note

The plan's revenue cost is **£37,500**, of which **£24,000** is the pilot (13 evening sessions plus 13 weeks of transport), **£6,500** covers up to four extra Youth Studio sessions a month for the quarter, **£4,200** is a targeted mailer, and **£2,800** is two days of training.

For the pilot, at the target of 50 visits per session, that is about 650 visits; at the 2024 baseline of 36.5 per session, it is about 475. The 60-attendee cap means the maximum measurable attendance is 780 visits over 13 sessions. The cost per visit therefore depends heavily on attendance, and the plan does not use a per-visit tariff to justify the spend. These figures are indicative, not a target or a tariff.

The youth expansion is **£6,500 for up to 12 additional sessions** (four a month across the three months of the quarter) if the partner can fill them — a stronger supply-side proposition than the round-1 description of two extra sessions a month (six over the quarter). The mailer is **£4,200** for a specific estate audience and a specific offer; its value depends on whether it adds reach beyond existing networks, which the registration source data will show. Training is a one-off **£2,800** to support access and welcome; it is not justified on attendance alone.

The lift is not in this plan and is not judged by this quarter's attendance. It is a separate access/compliance investment whose benefit remains after the quarter.
