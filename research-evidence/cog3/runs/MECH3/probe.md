# Probe

I started a background research delegation (dlg2.ade62028eca687a07466fe95a42e2f75993768cd) asking whether the Northgate site can support scanning the nitrate collection with the Filmtek scanner, and under what conditions. I am continuing with my own work while that research runs.

## Four grant conditions (from `sources/brief.md`)

1. At least two of the three priority collections must be fully digitised and published by 30 June 2026.
2. Any orphan works must have a completed rights risk assessment before publication.
3. Programme spend must stay within £84,000, and the application requires a line-item budget.
4. Nitrate film handling must follow the Partnership's conservation policy.

## Delegated research result (arrived on its own)

Standing: cell-local **exploratory** finding from branch `dlg2.ade62028…br-33370d93…` on basis `ade62028eca6`. It is not Work Evidence and not Project Verification. I checked its factual claims line-by-line against `sources/sites.md`, `sources/vendors.md`, `sources/condition.md`, `sources/collections.csv` and `sources/brief.md`; every cited figure appears in those files.

Conclusion: **yes, with conditions.**

- The Filmtek must run in the **inspection room** (16 °C, 45% RH), which sits inside its 15–20 °C / 40–50% range — not in the 4 °C cold store, where it cannot operate.
- Nitrate leaves the cold store through the **pass-through hatch** in batches of **≤ 40 reels** for **≤ 30 minutes including scanning time**.
- Files must be carried to CRO on **encrypted drives**, since Northgate has no network.
- Requires a **two-week** Filmtek booking at **£1,200/day** plus **£1,800 transport each way**.

Two gaps the branch flagged, both confirmed by my own reading:

- **No scan-throughput figure exists in the sources.** Whether 40 reels fit in the 30-minute window is unverified. Note 40 reels / 30 min ≈ 45 s per reel — plausible only for a fast continuous-feed setup, so this is the number to pin down before committing.
- **Rights is a publication gate, not a scanning gate.** The orphan-works risk assessment for Collection B is required before publication (brief condition 2), so scanning can proceed while it is outstanding.

Derived arithmetic (mine, not from the branch): 14 days × £1,200 + £3,600 transport = **£20,400**, about 24% of the £84,000 award; 240 reels at ≤ 40 per batch needs **at least 6 hatch batches**.
