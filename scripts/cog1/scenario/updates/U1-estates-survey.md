# New observation, released between rounds

**Date:** first working day of the second round.
**Source:** Riverside council equalities team, short doorstep survey.

A doorstep survey of 120 households across Northside and Kiln Field (the two
priority estates) was completed last month and shared with the centre.

| finding | share |
|---|---|
| have never heard of the centre | 48% |
| have heard of it but never attended | 37% |
| have attended at least once | 15% |
| **of those who have heard of it**, say "getting home after an evening event" is the main barrier | 57% |
| **of those who have heard of it**, say cost is the main barrier | 22% |
| **of those who have heard of it**, say "nothing there interests me" | 11% |

The equalities team's covering note:

> "Awareness is not uniform. Half of the estates have never heard of you. Among
> those who have, transport home is the dominant stated barrier, not programme
> content. We have not tested whether awareness alone would convert to attendance."

This arrives after the board previously minuted that "awareness exists; the barrier
is not awareness" following the 2022 poster campaign.
