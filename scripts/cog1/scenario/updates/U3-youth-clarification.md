# Clarification, released between rounds

**From the partner youth organisation, in writing, to the centre.**

> "We want to correct the record on the Youth Studio. Our own funding changed in
> June 2024 and we reduced delivery from four sessions a month to two. That is the
> whole reason the monthly *visit* figure fell: we ran half as many sessions. The
> number of different young people attending went up over the same period, from 71
> in January 2024 to 115 in March 2025, and we now hold a waiting list of 40.
>
> Interest has not fallen. Supply fell. We can supply staff for up to four extra
> sessions a month for the coming quarter at the same £6,500 we quoted, and we
> would use the waitlist first."

The interview note in which the youth worker speculated that "young people have
moved on" is withdrawn by the same organisation.
