# Two earlier interventions and what actually happened

## 1. Evening opening pilot (autumn 2023, 12 weeks)

**What was done:** the centre opened on Thursday evenings for talks and film, and
at the same time a paid late minibus ran from the Northside and Kiln Field estates
to the centre, returning after the event.

**What happened:** evening attendance was 38% higher than the same weeks the
previous year, and the majority of new evening attendees came from the two
priority estates. The evening programme was popular. Staff reported no incidents.

**How it ended:** the minibus funding ran out. The centre continued the evening
programme for a further six weeks **without** the minibus. Attendance in those
weeks fell back to roughly the pre-pilot level, and the new estate attendees
stopped coming. The evening programme was then suspended.

**What the front-of-house coordinator wrote at the time:**

> "The evenings worked. They stopped working when the bus stopped. We tested that
> by accident."

## 2. Borough-wide print and poster campaign (2022, one quarter)

**What was done:** £9,000 on posters and leaflets across the borough, plus a
mail-drop.

**What happened:** total attendance for the quarter was within normal seasonal
variation. The post-campaign survey found word of mouth remained the dominant
channel (64% of respondents). No measurable change in first-time attendance from
the priority estates.

**Conclusion recorded by the board:** awareness exists; the barrier is not
awareness. The board minuted: *"Do not repeat the poster spend without a specific
audience and a specific offer."*
