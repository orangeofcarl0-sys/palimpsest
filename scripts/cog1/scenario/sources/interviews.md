# Stakeholder interviews — February 2025

Four conversations, each about 40 minutes, recorded as notes and agreed with the
speaker. Names are roles, not people.

## 1. Youth worker (partner organisation, delivers the Youth Studio)

> "Numbers are down in the Studio and I think the young people have just moved on
> to other things. It's hard to compete with phones. We used to run it every week
> and now we run it less often, but that's because of staffing, not because they
> stopped coming — when we do run it, it fills up. I'd say interest is there."

## 2. Front-of-house coordinator

> "Most of our regulars drive. The two estates on the north side are a bus change
> and a walk away, and the last bus back is 19:10, so anything that finishes after
> seven is no use to them. That's the thing people ask about most."

## 3. Volunteer trustee (finance)

> "The board wants a plan it can actually pay for. We have a fixed envelope for the
> quarter and the council has already told us to expect a reduction. Anything that
> needs a new post or a building change needs to be costed properly and sequenced,
> not just wished for."

## 4. Long-standing member (attends Evening Talks)

> "I came to the evening talks for years. They stopped for a while last summer and
> I assumed the whole thing had ended — nobody told me. I only found out they were
> back because I bumped into someone. Your communication is the problem, not the
> programme."

## Interviewer's note

Two of the four speakers disagree about the same fact: the youth worker says the
Studio's decline is about young people losing interest, while the same speaker's
own sentence says the sessions now run less often and fill when they run.
