# Operating constraints (2025 season)

## Venue and licence

- The main hall holds 180 seated.
- The premises licence permits events until 23:00, **but** the accessible entrance
  and the passenger lift are out of service pending repair. Any public event that
  uses the first floor is therefore not permitted until the lift is returned to
  service.
- The lift repair is **capital work**: it needs a 6-week lead time from instruction
  to completion, and it must be finished before any first-floor programme can run.

## Access

- Two large social-housing estates (Northside and Kiln Field) are the centre's
  stated priority neighbourhoods.
- Both are two bus changes from the centre. The last service back leaves the
  centre stop at **19:10** on weekdays.
- There is no staffed car park after 20:00.

## Staffing

- 1.5 FTE front-of-house across the whole week.
- The Youth Studio is delivered by a partner youth organisation under a service
  agreement; the centre provides the room and the partner provides the staff.
- The centre has no events manager post. Evening opening requires a named
  responsible person on site, which the current rota does not staff.

## Season

- The plan covers **one quarter: 13 weeks**, beginning the first week of April.
- Quarter budget must be committed at the start; unspent funds are not carried.

## Reporting

- The council requires one quarterly report with attendance and equalities
  monitoring for the priority neighbourhoods.
