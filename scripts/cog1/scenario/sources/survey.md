# 2025 Visitor Satisfaction Survey

Issued by the trust's marketing volunteer. Summary written 2025-03-18.

## Headline

**92% of visitors are satisfied or very satisfied.** Satisfaction is high and stable.

## Method as written in the summary

> "The survey was handed to visitors at the Family Saturdays sessions across the
> free-entry weekend of 1–2 March 2025. 310 completed forms were returned. This
> gives us a robust picture of how our audiences feel about the centre."

## Tables

| question | result |
|---|---|
| overall satisfaction (satisfied or very satisfied) | 92% (285/310) |
| would recommend to a friend | 89% |
| programme "meets my needs" | 87% |
| heard about us through word of mouth | 64% |
| travelled by car | 71% |
| attended with children under 12 | 78% |

## Free-text themes (count of mentions)

- friendly staff (81)
- good value (57)
- parking (44)
- "wish there was more on in the evening" (12)
- café (9)
- "didn't know you ran other things" (6)

## Marketing volunteer's note at the bottom of the file

> "Board asked whether this covers everybody. It covers the people who come on a
> Saturday. We did not survey people who do not come, and the weekday and evening
> audiences were not in the room because the free-entry weekend was a family event."
