# Programme notes carried over

- 2024 nitrate pilot: 1,200 frames were scanned in the CRO imaging suite in July. Humidity rose to
  62% by the afternoon, condensation appeared on 14 frames, and the conservator stopped the run.
  Lesson: CRO is not suitable for nitrate in summer. Do not repeat the pilot unchanged.
