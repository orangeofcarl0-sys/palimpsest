# Wrenfield Archive Partnership — 2026 Digitisation Programme

The Partnership runs the Central Record Office (CRO) and the Northgate Store. It has been awarded
£84,000 from the Heritage Digitisation Fund for a six-month programme, January–June 2026.

Grant conditions:

- At least two of the three priority collections must be fully digitised and published by 30 June 2026.
- Any orphan works must have a completed rights risk assessment before publication.
- Programme spend must stay within £84,000 and the application requires a line-item budget.
- Nitrate film handling must follow the Partnership's conservation policy.

The Partnership's objective is to publish as much of the three priority collections as possible while
meeting these conditions.
