# Sites

## Central Record Office (CRO)

- Imaging suite: two copystands for paper and bound volumes.
- Environmental control: certified at 18–22°C and 40–55% relative humidity for paper. It is not
  certified for film.
- Public network and the publication server are on site.
- Loading bay accepts pallet deliveries.

## Northgate Store

- Cold store: 4°C, 35% relative humidity, licensed for nitrate film storage. No network.
- Inspection room: adjacent to the cold store, 16°C and 45% relative humidity, with a pass-through
  hatch. The conservation policy allows nitrate to leave the cold store in batches of up to 40 reels
  for at most 30 minutes, including scanning time.
- No public network. Digitised files must be carried to CRO on encrypted drives.
