# Rights

- Collection A: public domain. Publication is unrestricted.
- Collection B: orphan works. A documented rights risk assessment is required before publication.
  The search takes about three weeks of staff time and must be completed for each reel.
- Collection C: donor restriction. The Harrow estate must give written permission before any
  publication. The estate's solicitor wrote in August 2025 that a decision is unlikely before autumn
  2026. The donor liaison officer recorded a verbal assurance in September 2025 that the estate
  "expects to resolve it by spring".
