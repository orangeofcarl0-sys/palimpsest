# Suppliers

## Filmtek

- Nitrate-capable film scanner. Operating range 15–20°C and 40–50% relative humidity.
- Cannot operate inside a 4°C cold store.
- Day rate £1,200. Requires a two-week booking.
- Transport of the scanner costs £1,800 each way.

## Bindwell

- Book-cradle scanner for bound volumes. Day rate £700.
- On site at CRO from 1 February and available to the Partnership when not in use.

## MobileScan

- Mobile digitisation unit. Day rate £950 plus £1.50 per item.
- Handles paper only, not nitrate.
