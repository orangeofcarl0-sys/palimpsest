# Conservation notes

- Collection A: brittle bindings. Scan on a book cradle and never press the spines. About 12% of the
  1,800 volumes need stabilisation first; the conservator estimates six weeks for 220 volumes.
- Collection B: nitrate film. Keep items in the cold store when they are not being scanned. The
  pass-through hatch removes the need for acclimatisation. The film scanner must operate at 15–20°C
  and below 50% relative humidity.
- Collection C: mixed condition. About 8% is mould-affected and must be isolated and treated before
  scanning.
