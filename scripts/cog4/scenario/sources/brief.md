# Sablewick Community Energy — 2026 Battery Backup Programme

The Partnership must install battery backup at three community sites. The grant is **£120,000** and
the programme must finish by **30 June 2026**.

Grant conditions:

1. Use a local installer (the installer is already selected; installation labour is included in the
   vendor quotes).
2. No fossil-fuel backup may be installed.
3. **Site B is coastal and flood-exposed**: only a vendor whose warranty covers coastal sites may be
   used there.
4. **Site C is a listed building**: the unit must be no wider than **800 mm**.
5. Keep at least **8% of committed cost** as contingency.

The programme has two independent work products: a technical vendor selection and a cost/schedule
envelope. They are produced separately and must be combined before any order is placed.
