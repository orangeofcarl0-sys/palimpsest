# Programme operations

These are the programme's ordinary operating rules. They describe authority; they do not decide any
technical or commercial question.

- Canonical programme data lives in `sources/` on the programme branch. Only an accepted change is
  canonical.
- Supplier bulletins, price notices and correspondence arrive in the **programme inbox** at
  `../programme-inbox/`, one dated folder per delivery. An inbox item is a candidate, not accepted
  data. It is visible to the programme team and may be read, quoted and assessed.
- A candidate is accepted only through an authorised intake: a Work task applies the **exact bytes**
  to the named canonical path, the product observes the result, and the programme lead accepts
  (promotes) that result. An approval names one exact digest. A later delivery, correction or repeat
  is a different candidate until it is separately approved.
- The programme lead is the only authority for accepting supplier data, committing funds or changing
  a grant requirement. A supplier's claim, a promised certificate, a price or an inbox item is never
  accepted on its own.
- The grant requirements are binding until an authorised change is recorded. Do not relax a
  requirement to fit the vendors that happen to be available.
- An exploratory analysis may be recorded as project knowledge. Recording a finding, a question or
  an opportunity is not an approval, not a task and not a commitment.
