# Update — transport

Date: 12 January 2026

The town council has approved a **Friday late bus pilot**. From Friday 6 February 2026 the last
service from the square will leave at **21:15** instead of 18:40. The pilot is funded for six
months and covers Fridays only. The council will review it in July. There is no change to the
Saturday or Sunday service, and no extra service on other weekdays.

The council's transport officer writes: "This is a pilot, not a permanent commitment. We will look
at ridership after three months. If the bus is not used, it will not continue."
