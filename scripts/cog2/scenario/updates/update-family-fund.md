# Update — family fund

Date: 12 January 2026

The trustees have set the family fund at **£4,000 for January–June 2026**. The rate is the same
£180 per Saturday session as 2024. There are 26 Saturdays in the period, so a weekly childcare
offer would cost £4,680; the fund covers **22 sessions** (£3,960), leaving four Saturdays without
family-fund support. The trustees say the fund may also be used for other family support, so it
should not be assumed to be exclusively available to the Saturday workshop.

The trustees have also decided that any childcare offered from April 2026 must be advertised as
"supported by the family fund" and cannot be described as permanently free.
