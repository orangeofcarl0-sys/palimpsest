# Update — east hall repair

Date: 12 January 2026

The council has approved the capital grant for the east hall HVAC repair. The contractor will
complete the work between 9 and 27 February 2026. The hall is unavailable for public events during
that period. Commissioning is scheduled for 28 February, and the building officer expects the hall
to be usable for sensitive material **from 1 March 2026**.

The repair is paid from the capital grant and does **not** come out of the £60,000 programming
budget. The building officer adds: "Usable from 1 March is the plan, not a guarantee. Do not book
anything before the commissioning certificate is issued."
