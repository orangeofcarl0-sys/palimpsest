# Staff and volunteer interview notes (October–November 2025)

Names are initials.

## Transport

- JW (front of house): "The last minibus from the square is 18:40. After that it is a taxi, about
  £14 return, or a lift. The 2023 evening film nights ended because people could not get home, not
  because they disliked the films."
- RS (volunteer driver): "We looked into hiring a minibus for the three 2023 nights. The quote was
  £240 a night and the driver funding fell through, so the pilot ran without one. The council runs
  the scheduled service and has never extended it."
- AK (member, 68): "I would come to an evening talk if I could get home. I am not walking to the
  estate in the dark."

## Childcare

- MD (workshop lead): "Saturday mornings changed when we offered childcare. Families came for the
  workshop and stayed for the café. The childcare worker costs £180 per session, four hours,
  two staff. The family fund paid for it in 2024."
- MD: "The family fund is independent of the programming budget. It is capped at £9,000 for the
  first half of 2026, and the trustees have not yet decided whether it can be used for every
  Saturday."

## The east hall

- PB (building officer): "The east hall was recorded as climate-controlled in the 2019 survey.
  That is no longer true. The HVAC unit failed in January 2025. The repair quote is £18,000 and the
  unit cannot be run until it is repaired. The west hall has never been climate-controlled. The
  basement store is damp in winter and has no environmental control at all."
- PB: "A capital grant application is with the council. If it is approved, the repair would be
  scheduled for late February 2026 and the hall would be usable from 1 March."

## Programming

- LD (programmer): "The Tuesday craft group is loyal but ageing. The Thursday film club has fallen
  since 2024. The Saturday family workshop is the only session that grew."
- LD: "We have not tried a Sunday afternoon series. We have no data on it."

## Staffing

- Two full-time staff (director, programmer), one part-time administrator, and a pool of eight
  volunteers. Evening events need two staff present; the trust's licence permits events until
  23:00, but the building must be empty by 23:30.
