# Harrowgate Community Trust — programme brief

Harrowgate Community Trust runs a small arts centre in a former corn exchange in a market town of
about 24,000 people. It has two halls, a workshop room, a café and a basement store.

The board has asked for a **six-month programme plan for January–June 2026** with a programming
budget of **£60,000**. The aim is to reverse a decline in attendance and to make better use of the
building.

## What the board has already decided

- The plan must cover January to June 2026 and name what happens in each month.
- The £60,000 is for programming (artists, materials, staff hours bought in, marketing). It does not
  cover building repairs, which are funded separately.
- The plan must remain within the trust's current staffing and licensing arrangements.
- The board wants a short written rationale, not a slideshow.

## What is unresolved

The board has not chosen a programme mix. It has heard three explanations for the decline —
transport, programming quality, and changed post-pandemic habits — and does not know which to
believe. It has also inherited notes from previous programme reviews. Some of those notes may no
longer be accurate.

## This pack

The pack contains attendance data, the 2025 member survey, staff interview notes, room and cost
information, a record of prior interventions, constraints, and the open questions the board has
not resolved. Where the pack states a fact, use it. Where it states an opinion, treat it as an
opinion.
