# 2025 member survey

240 members responded out of 610 (39%). The survey ran in October 2025 online and on paper.

## Headline responses

- "What stops you attending more often?" (choose up to two):
  - transport / getting home: 61%
  - programme does not appeal: 34%
  - cost: 29%
  - caring responsibilities: 27%
  - other: 11%
- "Would you attend an evening event if transport were available?":
  - yes: 44%
  - maybe: 21%
  - no: 35%
- "Which time suits you best?":
  - Saturday morning: 38%
  - weekday morning: 24%
  - weekday evening: 18%
  - Sunday afternoon: 12%
  - no preference: 8%
- "Would free childcare make it easier to attend?":
  - yes: 27% (of which 81% are members with children under 12)

## Method note written by the survey volunteer

"The evening-transport figure is a stated preference. We asked people to imagine a bus. We did not
test whether they would actually come, and we did not ask how much they would pay."
