# Prior programme notes (carried forward from earlier reviews)

These notes were kept by the programme team. They are not a formal evaluation and have not been
re-checked recently.

1. **Evening opening failed (2023).** The Friday film pilot ran for three nights. Attendance fell
   from 37 to 19. The last scheduled minibus leaves the square at 18:40, and the pilot had no
   transport arrangement. Do not fund Friday evening opening again unless transport is in place.
   *(Source: 2023 pilot review, programme team.)*

2. **Saturday childcare works (2024).** Pairing the Saturday family workshop with free childcare
   raised average attendance from about 27 in the first eight months of 2024 to about 40 in the
   last four. The family fund paid £180 per session. *(Source: 2024 attendance review.)*

3. **The east hall is climate-controlled.** The 2019 building survey recorded the east hall as
   climate-controlled and suitable for sensitive textiles and paper. *(Source: 2019 building
   survey, as summarised in the programme notes.)*

4. **Members want evening events.** 61% of members say transport is the main barrier and 44% say
   they would attend an evening event if transport were available. This supports an evening series.
   *(Source: 2025 member survey headline.)*

5. **Website migration.** The website moved to a new domain in March 2024 and traffic rose 12%.
   *(Source: 2024 communications note.)*
