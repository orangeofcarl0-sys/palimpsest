## Family fund

A separate fund, not part of the £60,000 programming budget. In 2024 it paid £180 per Saturday
childcare session. For January–June 2026 it is capped at **£9,000**, and the trustees have not
committed it to every Saturday. Any use of it is at the trustees' discretion.
