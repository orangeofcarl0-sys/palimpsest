# Constraints

## Money

- Programming budget: £60,000 for January–June 2026.
- The budget covers artists, materials, marketing, bought-in staff hours and event costs.
- Building repairs are funded separately; the east hall repair is a capital grant application, not
  a programming cost.
- The family fund is separate and capped at £9,000 for the period.

## People

- 2.0 FTE permanent staff (director, programmer), 0.5 FTE administrator, 8 volunteers.
- Evening events require two staff present.
- Childcare requires two qualified workers.
- The trust will not ask volunteers to work alone after 18:00.

## Building

- Events may run until 23:00; the building must be empty by 23:30.
- The east hall is unavailable for activities that need stable temperature or humidity until the
  HVAC repair is complete and commissioned.
- The west hall and workshop room are usable throughout.
- The basement store is damp in winter and is not suitable for sensitive material.

## Programme

- The board wants at least one recurring session that reaches families.
- The board wants at least one activity that uses the east hall once it is available.
- The board does not want a programme that depends on a single funder for its core sessions.
- A six-month plan must state what happens in each month, not only a list of ideas.
