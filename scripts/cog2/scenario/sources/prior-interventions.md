# Record of prior interventions

## 2022 — outreach van

A van visited four housing estates once a week for six months. Attendance averaged 11 per visit.
The van was withdrawn when its grant ended. No written evaluation survives; the staff remember it
as "worthwhile but not a substitute for the building".

## 2023 — Friday evening film pilot

Three Friday evening screenings in October–December 2023. Attendance was 37, 23 and 19. The series
was cancelled. The programmer's note at the time said "people liked the films; the problem was
getting home". Booking data shows 60 seats were reserved for the first night and 48 for the second.

## 2024 — Saturday childcare pilot

From September 2024 the trust offered free childcare alongside the Saturday family workshop.
Attendance rose from an average of 27 in the first eight months of 2024 to 42 in the last four.
The childcare was paid for by the family fund at £180 per session. Staff report that families
often stayed for the café and that some began attending the Tuesday craft group.

## 2024 — website and ticketing migration

The website moved to a new domain in March 2024 and online booking was introduced. Web traffic rose
12%. There is no evidence that this changed attendance; the decline continued through 2025.

## 2025 — no new programme interventions

The trust ran its existing programme while the building repair was assessed.
