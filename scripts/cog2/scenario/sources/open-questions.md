# Open questions the board has not resolved

1. Is the decline mainly transport, programming, or changed habits? The survey and the interviews
   point in more than one direction.
2. Would an evening series work now, or did the 2023 pilot settle the question?
3. Is the 2019 "climate-controlled" description of the east hall still safe to rely on for a
   textile exhibition?
4. Should the Saturday childcare be offered every week, given the family fund cap?
5. Is a Sunday afternoon series worth testing? There is no local evidence either way.
6. How should the trust measure success in six months — attendance, revenue, new members, or
   something else?
