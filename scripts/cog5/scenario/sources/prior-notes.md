## 2023 Marsh Lane trial

A Kestrel (V2) unit was trialled at Marsh Lane in January 2023. It failed to start at **-8 °C**.
Do not specify V2 for a site whose design minimum is below -5 °C.

## Solaris (V3) certification

Solaris claims a cold-weather certificate for the LTO unit. The certificate has been promised but is
**not on file**. Treat the claim as unverified until the document is received.
